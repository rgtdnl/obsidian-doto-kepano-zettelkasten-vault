# 50 Templates

Templates set the `categories` link automatically, so creating a note from the right template is what puts it on the right dashboard.

## The templates

- **fleeting.md** — capture in `00 inbox`. Category [[fleeting]]. Three fates: main note, sleeping, delete.
- **reference.md** — one per source in `10 reference`. Category [[reference]].
- **main.md** — one idea + links in `20 slip-box`. Category [[main]]. Fill in the alphanumeric `id` (folgezettel).
- **hub.md** — entry points to trains of thought. Category [[hub]].
- **structure.md** — unpack a train of thought semantically. Category [[structure]].
- **keyword-index.md** — the serendipity oracle. Category [[index]].
- **project.md** — one per piece of writing in `30 projects`. Category [[project]].
- **creative-log.md** — the log beside each project. Category [[creative-log]].
- **journal.md** — the daily journal in `07 journal`. Category [[journal]].
- **my-note.md** — deliberately outside the system. Free-form notes that appear in no Base.

## Using a template

Right-click in the folder → "New note from template", or use the Templates command (Ctrl/Cmd + T). Delete the usage hints after filling.

## Editing the templates

The templates are ordinary notes; edit freely. Keep the frontmatter `categories` field pointing at the right category page, or the note will not appear on its dashboard.
