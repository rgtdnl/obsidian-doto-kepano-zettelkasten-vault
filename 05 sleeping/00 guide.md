# 05 Sleeping: the waiting room

**Purpose:** a quiet place for captures that keep getting passed over during processing, but still feel relevant to your thinking.

The book: any notes that seem hard to process, but are still relevant to your thinking, should be moved to a "Sleeping" folder.

## What lives here
- **Sleeping notes:** ideas not ready to become main notes. Not dead, just asleep.

## Rules
- Move captures here when they resist processing: no obvious connection to the slip box, no active project, or the idea just isn't as interesting as it was.
- Check in **every few months**.
- When an idea wakes up (a new connection found, a new project, renewed interest): promote it to a main note in `20 slip-box`, then delete the sleeping note.
- Never throw sleeping notes away on a whim. Their time may come.

## Quarterly action
1. Open `01 sleep.base`.
2. Read anything that has been sleeping for a while.
3. Promote what's ready, re-sleep what isn't.
