# 00 Inbox: the capture zone

**Purpose:** one flat landing zone for fleeting notes — quick captures of passing thoughts. What you see is what still needs work. Nothing here is finished.

The book: fleeting notes are reminders of ideas, captured as thoughts arise, never stored in the zettelkasten itself. They wait here until processed.

## What lives here
- **Fleeting notes:** thoughts, questions, jots, "ZK"-marked captures from journals. Format free. Frontmatter optional.

## Rules
- Process the inbox **at least once a week**.
- Every fleeting note has one of three fates:
  1. becomes a main note in `20 slip-box`,
  2. moves to `05 sleeping` (still interesting, not ready to connect),
  3. is deleted. Tasks, reminders, and lists are not ideas; they never enter the slip box.
- Fleeting notes are not stored in the zettelkasten. If it's still in here, it's not a note yet.
- One place only. No second notebook, no second app.

## Weekly action (10–15 min)
1. Open `01 queue.base`. Read everything in the inbox.
2. Worth keeping? Turn it into a main note (template: `main.md`), then delete the capture.
3. Interesting but not ready? Move it to `05 sleeping`.
4. Not worth keeping? Delete. Practice non-attachment.
5. Journals: write "ZK" next to zettel-worthy entries as you go; convert the marked ones when you process the inbox.
