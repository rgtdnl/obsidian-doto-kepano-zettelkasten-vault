# 40 Archive: finished work

**Purpose:** finished projects only. The final state of a piece of writing.

The book: what you don't use today can be used tomorrow. Finished writing is material for future writing.

## Rules
- Move finished projects here (set status to [[done]] first). Keep the subfolder intact: project note, draft, creative log.
- The slip box is never archived. Main notes stay in `20 slip-box` forever.
- Ideas from archived work can be pulled back into new projects at any time. The reference notes and main notes that fed the piece are still live.
