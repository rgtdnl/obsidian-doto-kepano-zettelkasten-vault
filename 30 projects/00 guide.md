# 30 Projects: writing from the slip box

**Purpose:** active writing, one subfolder per piece. Projects reference the slip box; they don't absorb it.

The book: writing emerges out of the system, and can also be incorporated back into it.

## What lives here
- **Project notes:** one per piece of writing (template: `project.md`). Status tracked from [[planned]] to [[done]].
- **Drafts:** the working document itself.
- **Creative logs:** the log for each piece (template: `creative-log.md`).

## Rules
- One subfolder per project: `30 projects/<name>/` holds the project note, the draft, and the creative log.
- **Task-focused project management:** define the project (active verb, end date) → brainstorm tasks → give tasks "do" dates → gather resources (zettelkasten, old articles, comments, posts, outlines) → do only today's tasks → record progress in the creative log.
- Work multiple projects per day, mixing focused, casual, and creative tasks.
- Finished projects move to `40 archive`. The slip box notes stay in `20 slip-box` forever.
- The daily journal (`07 journal`) is where each writing day starts: read yesterday, check the end-of-day check-in, open the recommended piece's creative log, then write.

## Daily action
1. Open the journal. Read yesterday's entry and the end-of-day check-in.
2. Open the creative log of the piece the check-in recommends.
3. Do today's tasks. Only today's.
4. Evening: record what you did in the creative log (done / needs attention / next), then journal the outcome and tomorrow's plan.
