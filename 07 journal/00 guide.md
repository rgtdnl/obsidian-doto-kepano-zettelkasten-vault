# 07 Journal: the central hub of the workday

**Purpose:** the daily journal. Interstitial logging during the day, an end-of-day check-in, suggestions for tomorrow.

The book: the daily journal is the central hub of my workday. It's where I record the day's activities, decide what writing to work on, and make suggestions on what to continue working on the following day.

## What lives here
- One file per year (`2026.md`, `2027.md`), with the latest day's entry at the top of the file.

## Rules
- **Interstitial journaling:** in the space between tasks, record what you completed, what you thought about it, what you're about to do, and what you think about doing it. Record procrastination breaks too; they reveal your workflow.
- Record conversations, tasks completed, and anything worth remembering (the book: "like when a contractor quotes me a price over the phone").
- **End of Day Check-In:** final thoughts plus suggestions for tomorrow, written to your future self. Write as if speaking to someone else, so the text feels personal and pertinent.
- **The writing loop:** morning → read yesterday's entry → check the check-in → open the recommended piece's creative log → the draft → write. Evening → record progress in the creative log, then journal the session's outcome and tomorrow's plan.

## Action
1. Create this year's file with the `journal.md` template.
2. Log interstitially through the day.
3. Close each day with the End of Day Check-In.
