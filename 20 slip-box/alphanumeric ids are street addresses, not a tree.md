# Alphanumeric IDs are street addresses, not a tree

Folgezettel IDs (1.1, 1.1a, 1.1a1...) mark that a relationship exists between notes, never what the relationship means. The occupant of 1A is not the sibling of the occupant of 1B, and the occupant of 1 Middle Rd is not the parent of the one at 2 Middle Rd. An ID is an address: it gets you to the idea and shows it was connected to something, but the nature of the connection lives in the note's text. This is why there are no "occupied slots" and no renumbering; folgezettel is not an outline.
