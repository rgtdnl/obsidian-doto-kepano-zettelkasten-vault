# The zettelkasten is a septic tank

Luhmann's word for his slip box: a place where notes do not need to be clarified, perfected, or current before they enter. What a note means, and whether it earns its place, is decided later, in relation to the notes that come after it. The box does the separating over time; the note maker does not need to pre-judge every idea at the door. This is permission to write imperfect notes, and to stop polishing an idea before it has met its neighbors.

## connects to
- extends: [[the value of an idea is determined by its relationships]] — if relationships decide value, then a note can enter half-formed; its meaning is completed by what it later touches.
- contradicts: the urge to edit old notes whenever new information refutes them. Wrong ideas, left in place and challenged by new notes, become material showing how thinking shifted.
