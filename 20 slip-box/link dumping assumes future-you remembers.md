# Link dumping assumes future-you remembers

"Link dumping" is dropping a link into a note without saying why the connection was made, trusting that the reason will be obvious later. It will not be. Months or years on, the relationship between a Sufi saying about gift-giving and a note on parasocial relationships is anything but self-evident. State the reason for every "see" link at the moment you make it; the few seconds of writing also deepen your understanding of the idea itself.

## connects to
- extends: [[the value of an idea is determined by its relationships]] — an uncontextualized link is a relationship with the context stripped out; the value leaks away.
- contradicts: the habit of collecting links as a to-do for later; if the context is not written now, it is lost.
